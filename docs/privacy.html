Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Wordout does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All game data — statistics, settings, preferences — is stored locally on your device only and never leaves your device.

🚫
No ads Zero ad SDKs. No ad networks. No sponsored content.
📊
No analytics No Firebase, no Mixpanel, no crash reporters, nothing.
🔍
No tracking The app has no idea who you are and no way to find out.
👤
No account required No sign-in, no email, no profile. Just open and play.
📱
Local storage only Your stats and settings live on your device. Uninstall and they're gone — no cloud backup, no server copy.

Questions?

Open an issue at github.com/dilippanicker/wordout — that's the only way to reach us, and it's intentionally public so answers benefit everyone.
