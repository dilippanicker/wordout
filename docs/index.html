Why Wordout

Built for players, not advertisers.

🚫

Zero ads, zero tracking

No ad SDKs, no analytics, no data collection. The app doesn't know who you are and doesn't need to.

📝

Curated word list

Answers are hand-curated from common English words. No obscure traps, no proper nouns. Spot a problem? Submit feedback on GitHub.

🌍

American & British English

Switch between US and UK word lists. COLOUR and COLOR are both valid — play in the English you actually use.

💪

Hard mode

Revealed hints must appear in every subsequent guess. For players who want the extra challenge.

🎨

Colour blind mode

Replaces green and yellow with high-contrast orange and blue. Because everyone should be able to play.

📖

Open source

Every line of code is on GitHub — word lists, game logic, everything. Fork it, audit it, improve it.
