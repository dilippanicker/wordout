**Full description**

```
Wordout is an open-source Wordle-style word puzzle game. Guess the hidden five-letter word on one board, or take on up to eight boards at once. A new puzzle arrives every day, the same for everyone. No ads, no sign-in, no server.

HOW TO PLAY
Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word. After each guess, the tiles reveal how close you are:

🟩 Green — right letter, right position
🟨 Yellow — right letter, wrong position
⬛ Grey — letter not in the word

New? An animated tutorial walks you through your first game automatically.

DAILY WORD
One new word every day, the same for everyone, but now in three difficulties. Win Easy to unlock Hard. Win Hard to unlock Extreme. Each difficulty uses a different word and tracks its own streak. Come back daily and chase all three.
No server picks the daily words. They're derived from the date using a deterministic hash. Same formula, same day, same words, for everyone.

MULTI-BOARD MODE
Use the ◄ ► arrows or swipe left/right to switch between board counts:
Wordout classic: single board, 6 guesses
2-out:  2 words, 7 guesses
3-out:  3 words, 8 guesses
4-out:  4 words, 9 guesses
6-out:  6 words, 11 guesses
8-out:  8 words, 13 guesses
Every guess applies to all boards at once. The indicator row shows your progress across all boards at a glance.

DIFFICULTY
🐣 Easy: no restrictions
💪 Hard: revealed hints must be used in all future guesses
💀 Extreme: fewer guesses, more pressure

FEATURES

American and British English word lists
Haptic feedback on every guess and win
Tap any tile to clear it and everything to its right
Color blind mode  has high-contrast orange and blue
Dark and light theme
Stats and guess distribution per mode and difficulty
Share your results as an emoji grid

WORD LIST
Every answer is carefully curated, no random dictionary dumps, no obscure words, no proper nouns, no plurals. Just clean, everyday English words you'll actually know.

PRIVACY
No ads. No accounts. No analytics. No internet connection required. Stats stored only on your device.
Open source on GitHub: https://github.com/dilippanicker/wordout
```
